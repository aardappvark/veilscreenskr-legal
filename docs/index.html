What is VeilScreenSkr?

VeilScreenSkr is a software-based screen privacy protection application for the Solana Seeker device. It applies layered visual overlay filters to make your screen harder to read for people glancing from the side or at a distance, helping protect against casual shoulder-surfing in public spaces like trains, cafes, and offices.

Features

Layered Filters

Three independent filters — Dimmer, Noise, and Pattern — that work together or individually for customised protection.

Boost Mode

Maximum intensity mode for high-exposure situations. Free for SGT holders, pay-per-session for others.

Quick Presets

One-tap filter profiles for Commute, Office, and Night scenarios.

Seeker Integration

Sign In With Solana, SGT verification, Quick Settings tile, and home screen widget.

Important Notice

VeilScreenSkr is a software-only privacy tool. It does not replicate hardware privacy screen filters. The overlay cannot physically control the direction of light from your screen. It is most effective against casual glancing in public spaces.
