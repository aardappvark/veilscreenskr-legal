2. Description of Service

VeilScreenSkr is a software-only screen privacy protection application that applies visual overlay filters to make your screen harder to read by nearby observers. The App uses the Android SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW overlay permission to draw visual filters on top of your screen content.

Key features include:

  • Three independent overlay filters (Dimmer, Noise, and Pattern)
  • Quick presets for common scenarios (Commute, Office, Night)
  • Boost Mode for maximum-intensity privacy protection
  • Optional wallet connection for Seeker platform identity and SGT verification
  • Quick Settings tile and home screen widget

3. Limitations of Software-Based Privacy

VEILSCREENSKR IS A SOFTWARE-ONLY PRIVACY TOOL. IT IS NOT A HARDWARE PRIVACY SCREEN FILTER AND DOES NOT REPLICATE THE FUNCTIONALITY OF ONE.

You acknowledge and agree that:

  • The App applies visual overlays only — it cannot physically control the direction of light emitted by your screen
  • The overlay is purely visual and does not capture, read, record, or transmit any screen content beneath it
  • Software overlays are inherently less effective than hardware privacy filters at restricting viewing angles
  • The App is most effective against casual glancing and shoulder-surfing in public spaces
  • No software-based solution can guarantee complete screen privacy in all conditions
  • Effectiveness varies depending on screen brightness, ambient lighting, viewing distance, and overlay intensity settings

See our Disclaimer & Risk Disclosure for full details on the limitations of software-based screen privacy.
