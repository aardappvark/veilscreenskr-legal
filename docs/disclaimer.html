Effective: February 2026

WARNING: VeilScreenSkr is a SOFTWARE-ONLY screen privacy tool. It does NOT replicate the functionality of hardware privacy screen filters and CANNOT physically control the direction of light emitted from your screen. VeilScreenSkr provides convenience-level visual privacy only and is NOT a security product. No guarantee of complete privacy protection is made. You use VeilScreenSkr entirely at your own risk.

1. Nature of the Application

VeilScreenSkr ("the App") is a software-only screen privacy overlay designed for the Solana Seeker device. The App applies visual overlays and filter effects to reduce the readability of on-screen content when viewed from off-axis angles.

  • VeilScreenSkr is not a hardware privacy screen filter and does not replicate the optical properties of physical privacy films or screens.
  • The App cannot physically control, redirect, or limit the direction of light emitted from your device's display.
  • VeilScreenSkr is not a security product. It provides convenience-level visual privacy only.

2. Effectiveness Limitations

The effectiveness of VeilScreenSkr varies significantly depending on multiple factors, including but not limited to:

  • Ambient lighting conditions (bright sunlight, dim indoor lighting, artificial light sources)
  • The viewing angle of the observer relative to the screen
  • The distance between the observer and the device
  • The specific filter settings and overlay configuration chosen by the user
  • Screen brightness and display characteristics of the device
  • The type of content displayed on screen (contrast, colors, text size)

VeilScreenSkr is best suited for protection against casual shoulder-surfing in everyday environments. It is NOT guaranteed to prevent determined, deliberate, or sustained viewing by a motivated observer.
