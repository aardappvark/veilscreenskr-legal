Privacy Policy — VeilScreenSkr

Effective Date: July 2026

1. Overview

VeilScreenSkr ("the App") is published by MidMightBit Games, based in Melbourne, VIC, Australia. The App is a software-based screen privacy protection tool for the Solana Seeker device. It applies visual overlay filters to help protect your screen from casual shoulder-surfing in public spaces.

VeilScreenSkr is built with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect personal data, we use no third-party analytics SDKs or advertising trackers, and we serve no advertisements. Your settings and wallet data remain on your device. The App does send anonymous, aggregate usage counts — containing no personal data — to our own privacy-preserving analytics service, described in Section 8a.

2. Legal Basis for Data Processing

Under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and UK GDPR, the legal basis for processing your wallet public address (if you choose to connect a wallet) is legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f)) — specifically, the legitimate interest in providing SGT verification and boost session access that you voluntarily opt into by connecting your wallet. This processing is minimal, expected by the user, and proportionate.

Under the Australian Privacy Act 1988 and Australian Privacy Principles (APPs), the wallet public address is collected in accordance with APP 3 (collection of solicited personal information) for the primary purpose of on-chain identity verification and feature access.

3. Data We Store Locally

Filter Preferences (Local Only)

  • Dimmer intensity setting
  • Noise intensity setting
  • Pattern intensity setting
  • Active filter combination and preset selections
  • Stored in standard SharedPreferences on your device

App Settings (Local Only)

  • Haptic feedback preference
  • Auto-activate preference
  • Schedule configuration (time-based activation)
  • Stored in standard SharedPreferences on your device

Wallet Public Address (Optional, Encrypted)

  • If you connect a Solana wallet, your public address is stored locally on your device
  • Encrypted using Android EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256-GCM for values, AES-256-SIV for keys), backed by the Android Keystore
  • Used solely for SGT verification and boost session payments
  • This is a public key only — we never request, access, or store your private keys

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers
  • Personal or device-level usage analytics (we collect only anonymous, aggregate event counts — see Section 8a)
  • Crash reports
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Browsing history
  • Contact lists
  • Screen content, screenshots, or display captures
  • Private keys or seed phrases
  • Payment card details or bank information
  • Cookies or similar tracking technologies

5. SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW Permission

VeilScreenSkr requires the SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW (Draw Over Other Apps / Overlay) permission to function. This permission is essential because the App works by drawing a visual overlay filter on top of your screen to reduce readability from side angles.

Important: The overlay is purely visual. It applies dimming, noise, and pattern layers to the display output. It does not capture, record, read, or transmit any screen content. The overlay cannot access the content of other apps, and no pixel data from the underlying screen is ever processed or stored.

6. Wallet Connection (Optional)

VeilScreenSkr supports optional wallet connection via Sign In With Solana (SIWS) using the Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA). Wallet connection enables:

  • SGT Verification: Checking on-chain ownership of a Seeker Genesis Token to unlock free boost sessions
  • Boost Payments: Processing optional SOL or SKR payments for boost sessions (for non-SGT holders)

The MWA protocol operates locally between apps on your device. No wallet data is transmitted to external servers by MidMightBit Games. We store only your wallet public address (encrypted). We never have access to your private keys or the ability to sign transactions on your behalf without your explicit approval in your wallet app.

7. Payments and Transactions

VeilScreenSkr offers optional boost sessions that can be purchased with SOL or SKR tokens. These transactions are processed entirely on the Solana blockchain:

  • You approve each transaction individually in your wallet app
  • Transaction details are recorded on the public Solana blockchain
  • MidMightBit Games does not store payment details, transaction history, or financial records on our servers
  • We do not process credit cards, bank transfers, or fiat currency

8. Network Requests

The App makes the following network requests:

  • Solana RPC Endpoint: To verify SGT ownership on-chain. Your wallet public address is sent as part of the query. This is publicly available blockchain data.
  • Mobile Wallet Adapter (MWA): Local device-to-device communication between VeilScreenSkr and your wallet app. This does not leave your device.
  • Anonymous Usage Analytics: Aggregate event counts to our own Vercel service. No personal data is transmitted (see Section 8a).

All network communications use HTTPS/TLS encryption in transit. The App enforces HTTPS for all connections and does not permit cleartext traffic.

8a. Anonymous Usage Analytics

VeilScreenSkr sends anonymous, aggregate usage events to our own privacy-preserving analytics service — a Vercel serverless function with a Vercel Blob store that we operate. Each event records only:

  • the event name (for example, "app opened" or "shield activated");
  • the date; and
  • an approximate country that Vercel derives at its network edge from the connection.

Your IP address is never logged or stored by us. No user or device identifier, no wallet address, no filter settings, and no screen content are ever transmitted. Only aggregate counts — how many times each event happened, per country, per day — are retained. We use these counts solely to understand how many people use the App and where, so we can prioritise improvements.

Because this data is anonymous and aggregate, it is not personal data under GDPR Recital 26 and equivalent laws, and no consent is required. VeilScreenSkr uses no third-party analytics SDKs and no advertising trackers, requires no account, and does not build a profile of you.

9. IP Address Disclosure

The App does not collect or store your IP address. However, when the App makes network requests to Solana RPC endpoints, your IP address is transmitted to those services as part of standard internet protocol. MidMightBit Games has no access to any logs or data collected by third-party RPC providers. We encourage you to review their respective privacy policies.

10. Data Storage and Security

All personal data is stored locally on your device. Aside from the anonymous, aggregate analytics events described in Section 8a, no data is transmitted to any server owned or operated by MidMightBit Games. Security measures include:

  • Wallet public address encrypted with Android EncryptedSharedPreferences (AES-256)
  • Encryption keys secured by the Android Keystore
  • HTTPS enforced for all network connections
  • No cloud storage of personal data; our analytics service (Section 8a) retains only anonymous, aggregate counts
  • allowBackup is set to false in the App manifest to prevent wallet data from being included in device backups

11. No Accounts Created

VeilScreenSkr does not create user accounts. There is no registration, no login system, and no user profiles. Wallet connection via SIWS is optional and used solely for SGT verification and boost payments. Disconnecting your wallet removes all wallet-related data from your device.

12. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party libraries:

  • Solana Mobile Wallet Adapter: Used to connect to your Solana wallet app on-device. No data is sent to Solana Mobile.

The App does NOT include any:

  • Analytics SDKs
  • Advertising SDKs
  • Crash reporting SDKs
  • Social media SDKs
  • Push notification services
  • Cloud storage services

13. Data Retention

Your data is retained locally on your device for as long as the App is installed. Data is automatically deleted when you:

  • Disconnect your wallet within the App
  • Clear the App's data through Android Settings > Apps > VeilScreenSkr > Clear Data
  • Uninstall the App

Filter preferences and app settings are removed when the App is uninstalled or its data is cleared.

14. Cross-Border Data Transfers

When making RPC calls, your wallet public address and IP address are transmitted to Solana RPC endpoints to retrieve on-chain data. Your wallet public address is already publicly visible on the Solana blockchain. No user-specific data is sent to MidMightBit Games servers; our only server — the analytics service in Section 8a — receives anonymous, aggregate counts that contain no personal data.

We rely on the public nature of blockchain data and your explicit request to use this service as the basis for these transmissions. For EU/UK users, this processing is necessary for the performance of the service at your request (GDPR Article 49(1)(b)).

The only data sent to a server operated by MidMightBit Games is the anonymous, aggregate analytics described in Section 8a, which contains no personal data. Our analytics runs on Vercel's global network; Vercel acts as a processor and derives an approximate country at its edge without our code ever receiving or storing your IP address.

15. Your Rights

Right to Delete (Erasure)

You can delete all App data at any time by:

  1. Disconnecting your wallet within the App to remove wallet data
  2. Going to Android Settings > Apps > VeilScreenSkr > Clear Data to remove all local data
  3. Uninstalling the App

This satisfies the right to erasure under GDPR Article 17, UK GDPR, and the Australian Privacy Act.

Right to Access

All data stored by the App is visible within the App itself (filter settings, app settings, and wallet connection status). You may also contact us to request a summary of any data associated with your wallet address.

Right to Rectification (GDPR Article 16)

As the only externally-sourced data is your wallet public address from the Solana blockchain, the App displays factual on-chain data. If you believe any data is inaccurate, you can disconnect and reconnect your wallet to refresh on-chain data.

Right to Restriction of Processing (GDPR Article 18)

You may restrict the processing of your wallet address at any time by disconnecting your wallet. This stops all wallet-related data processing and removes your wallet address from local storage. You may reconnect at any time to resume the service.

Right to Object

You may object to the processing of your wallet address at any time by disconnecting your wallet, which removes your wallet address from local storage.

Right to Portability

As all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over your data at all times. Your wallet public address is the only externally-sourced data element and is already in your possession.

Right to Lodge a Complaint

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority:

  • Australia: Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (OAIC) — oaic.gov.au
  • EU/EEA: Your local Data Protection Authority
  • UK: Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) — ico.org.uk
